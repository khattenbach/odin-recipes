Description

BBQ Chicken Pizza features a crispy crust topped with smoky barbecue sauce, tender grilled chicken, and melted Colby-Jack cheese. It delivers the perfect balance of sweet, smoky, and savory in every bite.

Ingredients

  • 1 (12 inch) pre-baked pizza crust
  • 1 cup spicy barbeque sauce
  • 2 skinless boneless chicken breast halves, cooked and cubed
  • 1 cup sliced pepperoncini peppers
  • 1 cup chopped red onion
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 cups shredded Colby-Jack cheese

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Place pizza crust on a baking sheet. Spread barbeque sauce on crust.
  3. Top with chicken, pepperoncini peppers, onion, and cilantro.
  4. Cover with Colby-Jack cheese.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until cheese is melted and bubbly, about 15 minutes.

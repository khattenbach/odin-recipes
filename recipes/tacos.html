Description

Tacos are a traditional Mexican meal, consisting of a tortilla filled with meat, cheese, lettuce, tomatoes, and salsa. They are quick, easy to make, and customizable providing an ideal meal for dinners and gatherings.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup ranch dressing
  • 1/4 cup reduced-fat sour cream
  • 1 (1 ounce) packet taco seasoning mix, divided
  • 1 tablespoon mild chunky salsa
  • 2 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
  • 8 (6 inch) corn tortillas
  • shredded luttuce
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • 1 (4 ounce) can sliced black olives
  • 1 avocado - peeled, pitted and diced (Optional)
  • 1 cup shredded Colby-Monterey Jack cheese

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Combine ranch dressing, sour cream, 1 teaspoon taco seasoning, and salsa in a small bowl. Cover and regrigerate until serving.
  3. Toss chicken with remaining taco seasoning. Cover bowl loosely with wax paper or plastic wrap. Microwave chicken until chicken is heated through, about 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Warm tortillas in a skillet for about a minute on each side to make them pliable.
  5. Place a scoop of chicken on the tortilla and top with lettuce, tomato, green onion, olives, avocado, cheese, and a spoonful of the ranch dressing mixture.
